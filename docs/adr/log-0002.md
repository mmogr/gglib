## 2026-10-15: re-measured at the b11429 bump, and both #755 bugs still reproduce

The pin moved from `b10327` (`69bf643`) to `b11429` (`d81235049`, which
upstream also named `v0.6.0`). The Method's harness was rerun at both builds
with the same files.

**Scope.** One machine, an Apple M4 Max with 128 GB, on Metal. Raw
llama-server, `--jinja -c 8192 -ngl 99`, with gglib out of the path.
`scripts/experiments/lazy_grammar_conformance.py` at its defaults (6 prompts ×
5 samples × 2 arms, temperature 1.0, `max_tokens` 1024). The models are Q8_0,
not the UD-Q6_K_XL files of the Method: `unsloth/Qwen3.5-4B-GGUF`
`Qwen3.5-4B-Q8_0.gguf` (`--dialect xml`) and `bartowski/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-GGUF`
`Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct-Q8_0.gguf` (`--dialect json`). The outputs are on the
owner's machine, not in the repository.

| model | build | `auto` | `required` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.5-4B | `b10327` | 30/30 | 30/30 |
| Qwen3.5-4B | `b11429` | 30/30 | 30/30 |
| Llama 3.2 3B | `b10327` | 4/30 (26 `max_lines` type) | 30/30 |
| Llama 3.2 3B | `b11429` | 5/30 (24 `max_lines` type, 1 HTTP 500) | 30/30 |

Llama's `auto` is still unconstrained and its `required` still enforced, so
findings 4 and 5 hold at the new pin.

**#755's reproducers**, the draft's curl at temperature 0.7, five attempts
each:

- (a) JSON dialect, a `path` of `{"a": "b"}`, on Llama 3.2 3B. At `b10327`:
  3 × HTTP 500, 1 × `path` `{`, 1 × exact. At `b11429`: 4 × HTTP 500 ("The
  model produced output that does not match the expected peg-native format"),
  and 1 × a `path` sent as an object. The server logs
  `unparsed peg-native output: {"name": "read_file", "parameters": {"path": "{"a": "b"}", "mode": "text"}}`.
- (b) XML dialect, a `path` of `a</parameter>b`, on Qwen3.5-4B. 5 of 5 at
  both builds return `{"path":"a</parameter>\n<parameter=mode>\ntext","mode":"text"}`.

Both still fail at the pin. The (b) case was reported upstream as
ggml-org/llama.cpp#26763 and closed NOT_PLANNED on 2026-08-09. Nothing was
filed for (a).
