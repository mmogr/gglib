# ADR 0018 log

The log of ADR 0018, image generation with stable-diffusion.cpp, which is not
written yet: the arc is open, and the ADR is written from this log when the
arc closes. Readings, measurements and interim decisions, appended and never
edited; a line number refers to the commit the entry names. Each entry
describes the code at the commit it lands in, so a later entry may change
what an earlier one says.

## 2026-10-09: stable-diffusion.cpp draws correctly on a Mac, and slowly

The measurement the arc starts from. It asked whether stable-diffusion.cpp
(sd.cpp) can be gglib's image runtime on the owner's Mac: whether it draws
correctly on Metal, how long an image takes, and what it does beside a chat
model that is already loaded.

**Scope.** One machine, an Apple M4 Max with 128 GB, macOS 27.0.1. sd.cpp at
`228c707`, built with `-DSD_METAL=ON`. One prompt and seed 42 for every arm.
The harness, logs and images are in a folder on the owner's machine and not
in the repository, so none of these numbers can be re-derived from a
checkout. The reproducer is the shape of the runs: `sd-cli` with
`--diffusion-model <weights>` and one flag per component (`--vae`,
`--clip_l`, `--t5xxl` for Flux; `--vae`, `--llm` for Qwen-Image 2.1), or `-m`
for the SDXL checkpoint, timed by sd.cpp's own log lines, with peak memory
from `/usr/bin/time`'s maximum resident set. Every arm ran with its files
already in the page cache. No CUDA or Vulkan machine, no Flux dev or Flux.2,
no LoRA, img2img or edit path was read.

The q8_0 and SDXL arms' weights and every component are the files whose
tensor tables are committed as goldens; [their
README](../../crates/gglib-core/src/domain/testdata/image_families/README.md)
records each file's repository, path, size and sha256. The Flux fp16 arm's
and both q4_k arms' weights are not among them.

**Readings.** Flux schnell at 4 steps, cfg 1.0, `euler`. SDXL base at 20
steps, cfg 7, `euler_a`, sd.cpp's default and what its log records.
Qwen-Image 2.1 at 20 steps, cfg 6, `euler`, with `--fa`, the settings of
sd.cpp's guide (`docs/qwen_image_2.1.md`).

| Arm | Size | Sampling | Per step | Decode | Total | Peak RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flux schnell q8_0 | 1024² | 66.5 s | 16.6 s | 7.5 s | 76.4 s | 22.9 GB |
| Flux schnell q4_k | 1024² | 66.2 s | 16.5 s | 7.4 s | 76.0 s | 16.8 GB |
| Flux schnell fp16 (safetensors) | 1024² | 79.0 s | 19.7 s | 7.5 s | 88.9 s | 34.1 GB |
| Flux schnell q8_0 with `--diffusion-fa` | 1024² | 65.8 s | 16.5 s | 7.4 s | 79.8 s | 22.9 GB |
| Flux schnell q8_0 | 512² | 17.6 s | 4.4 s | 2.0 s | 22.0 s | 23.5 GB |
| SDXL base fp16 | 1024² | 53.6 s | 2.7 s | 7.6 s | 62.4 s | 7.4 GB |
| Qwen-Image 2.1 q8_0 | 1024² | 536.0 s | 26.8 s | 9.5 s | 547.6 s | 16.5 GB |
| Qwen-Image 2.1 q4_k | 1024² | 578.6 s | 28.9 s | 9.5 s | 590.2 s | 13.5 GB |
| Qwen-Image 2.1 q8_0 | 512² | 126.8 s | 6.3 s | 2.5 s | 131.4 s | 17.4 GB |

- **Every quantization of Flux schnell drew a correct picture**, near-identical
  across them, with every weight on the Metal device and no fallback to the
  CPU.
- **Quantization did not change the speed.** q8_0 and q4_k are within 0.5% of
  each other; fp16 is 19% slower. q8_0 is the default this measurement
  recommends: the speed of q4_k and the picture of fp16, in a smaller file
  than fp16's.
- **Flash attention changed nothing** for Flux (65.8 s against 66.5 s), and
  512² took about a quarter of the time of 1024², so the cost follows the
  matrix sizes and not attention.
- **Decode is a fixed 7.4 to 7.6 s at 1024²** in every Flux and SDXL arm: the
  VAE on Metal.
- **Qwen-Image 2.1 is about nine minutes an image at 1024².** With guidance at
  6 every step is two passes, 40 passes in all. Its q4_k was slower than its
  q8_0.

**Beside a chat model.** Qwen3.8-27B at Q8 was resident in the owner's gglib
daemon, and three 256-token completions were sent to it while Flux q8_0
sampled a 1024² image.

| | Alone | Together |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen tokens per second | 15.3, 14.7 | 10.8, 9.9, 9.5 |
| Flux sampling | 65.8 s | 119.6 s |

Both finished, and nothing swapped.

**The server.** `sd-server` with Flux q8_0 and `--diffusion-fa`:

- It was ready in 2 s, and `GET /v1/models` answers one fixed id,
  `sd-cpp-local`: one process is one model, as with llama-server.
- `POST /v1/images/generations` answers OpenAI's shape,
  `{created, data: [{b64_json}], output_format}`, synchronously: 22.8 s end
  to end at 512², 66.0 s at 1024².
- Its errors are not OpenAI's shape: a missing prompt is a 400 with
  `{"error":"prompt required"}`. A size of `7x7` was not refused; it answered
  200 with an 8x8 image.

**Against MLX.** mflux 0.22.0 with the community 8-bit Flux schnell weights,
same prompt and seed, 4 steps, took 38.2 s and 38.5 s wall to wall at 1024²
in two runs (Python start, load, sampling and decode), 7.7 to 9.3 s a step
across them, with a peak of 14.0 to 15.1 GB, and 12.2 s at 512². Its 8-bit is
MLX's own quantization, so the weights are not those of the sd.cpp arms. The
measurement kept sd.cpp as the one runtime: MLX halves a wait nobody watches,
at the cost of a second runtime with a Python environment, another weight
layout and no OpenAI server of its own.

## 2026-10-10: gglib knows an image model by its tensors, and fetches its family

Interim decisions of the change that teaches the library what an image model
is: its family, the files it draws with beside its weights, a download that
brings them, and the surfaces that link them. Nothing here launches sd.cpp.
The code is `crates/gglib-core/src/domain/image_family.rs`,
`crates/gglib-core/src/services/model_links.rs`,
`crates/gglib-core/src/ports/huggingface/image_companions.rs` and
`crates/gglib-gguf/src/tensor_table.rs` at the commit this entry lands in;
"before" means `fe813ae4`.

**An image GGUF holds no metadata.** The two image GGUFs measured hold zero
key-value pairs; their tensor tables end at byte 53,912 (Flux schnell q8_0,
776 tensors) and 20,499 (Qwen-Image 2.1 Q8_0, 265), per the goldens' README.
Before, `validate_and_parse_gguf` checked the extension and a parse only, and
the parser read the tensor count and discarded it
(`crates/gglib-gguf/src/parser.rs:44-49` at `fe813ae4`), so `gglib model add`
registered Flux schnell as a chat model. Now `GgufParserPort` reads a file's
tensor table, from a GGUF or a safetensors header, and the parser reads on
into it to set the file's family, which the model keeps as `image_family`. A
table that cannot be read is logged and leaves no family; it never fails a
parse.

**The family is sniffed by sd.cpp's own rules.** `ImageFamily::sniff` ports
sd.cpp's loader (`src/model_loader.cpp` at `228c707`: Qwen-Image 2.1 by
`txt_in.text_norm.weight` at `:492-493`; Flux.2 at `:511`; Ovis at `:520`;
Flux's Fill, Controls, Flex.2 and LongCat by input widths at `:659-672`),
after stripping one leading `model.diffusion_model.` or `diffusion_model.`,
the prefix sd.cpp gives a `--diffusion-model` file
(`src/pipeline/diffusion_engine.cpp:734` at `228c707`). It knows three
families, `flux1`, `sdxl` and `qwen-image-2.1`, and answers no family for the
variants sd.cpp loads differently. A component's file is checked by
`ComponentRole::fits` with exact names, never stripped, so an SDXL
checkpoint's own VAE and text encoder never pass for separate files. The
eight goldens under `crates/gglib-core/src/domain/testdata/image_families`
are the evidence; they were cut from header bytes only, by the script their
README holds.

**One link rule, and the projector keeps its check.** A projector and a
component are linked through one function, `checked_link`. The projector is
still checked by one GGUF header read and nothing else, as ADR 0015 decision 1
says, with its refusals word for word as before; only a component's file is
read for its tensor table. A component is linked only to a model that has a
family, and only in a role that family's recipe names.

**The recipes, and where their sources come from.** One static table,
`ImageFamily::recipe`:

| | Flux.1 | SDXL | Qwen-Image 2.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main file | diffusion model only | everything (`-m`) | diffusion model only |
| VAE | `unsloth/FLUX.1-schnell` `ae.safetensors` | none | `Comfy-Org/Qwen-Image-2.1` `vae/qwen_image_2.1_vae_bf16.safetensors` |
| Text encoders | `comfyanonymous/flux_text_encoders` `clip_l.safetensors`, `t5xxl_fp16.safetensors` | none | `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct-GGUF` `Qwen3VL-8B-Instruct-Q8_0.gguf` as `llm` |
| Steps, cfg, sampler | 4, 1.0, `euler` | 20, 7.0, `euler_a` | 20, 6.0, `euler`, flash attention on |
| Size step, bounds | 64, 256 to 1536 | 64, 256 to 1536 | 32, 256 to 1536 |

Each source's path and size were checked against the Hub's
`paths-info/main` on 2026-10-09, a live reading taken outside the repository.
The Flux VAE the measurement used came from `black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-schnell`,
which answered 401 without a token (`curl -I` on its resolve address,
2026-10-09); `unsloth/FLUX.1-schnell` serves a file with the same LFS sha256,
ungated, and is the recipe's source. SDXL's sampler is `euler_a` because that
is what the measured run logged. sd.cpp aligns a size up to a multiple of
its VAE's scale times its diffusion model's down factor
(`src/pipeline/request.cpp:194-215` at `228c707`), and answered a 7x7
request for Flux with an 8x8 image; Flux's step of 64 is a safe start, not
sd.cpp's own. The size rule is data in this change and enforced by none of
it.

**Flux dev is Flux.1.** Dev carries `guidance_in.*` tensors, which the
schnell golden lacks and the sniff does not read, so a Flux dev file is
`flux1` and takes schnell's 4 steps. No dev file was measured. Steps per
model rather than per family are deferred.

**The family is known before the download.** A download reads the first
1 MiB of the first weights file (`SNIFF_HEAD_BYTES`) with a Range request,
sniffs it, and looks each companion of the recipe up by its path on the Hub.
A chat GGUF's header runs past that head (the Qwen3-VL GGUF's ends at
5,957,704 bytes, per the goldens' README), so its table cannot be read from
the head, and a chat model reads as no family and downloads as before. The
companions ride the model's one download group, under its id, so their bytes
ride the existing download events; each lands in its own repository's folder
of the models directory, where a second model of the family finds it
present. With no quantization asked, an image repository
is fetched at Q8_0 when it has it, after the measurement's q8_0
recommendation. The HuggingFace preview makes the same head read, of the
Q8_0 quantization's first file or else the smallest's, and shows the
companions, their sizes, which are already here, and the bytes a download
adds.

**What a companion's row holds, and repair.** A linked companion's
`model_files` row holds its absolute canonical path. Verification reads it
as it is; the update check finds no listing for it and skips it. A repair
never deletes a file a model links as a component, the twin of the
projector's rule, and when only components are unhealthy it says to link
another with `gglib model update <id> --component <role>=<path>`.

**An image search.** An image search asks the Hub for
`filter=gguf&filter=text-to-image` where a chat search asks for
`library=gguf&pipeline_tag=text-generation`. Probed on 2026-10-09, live and
outside the repository: the GGUF library with the text-to-image pipeline
answered diffusers repositories holding no GGUF file
(`stabilityai/stable-diffusion-xl-base-1.0`, `black-forest-labs/FLUX.1-dev`),
and the two tags answered GGUF image repositories only (unsloth's and
city96's Flux, Qwen-Image 2.1). Neither finds `leejet/FLUX.1-schnell-gguf`,
which has no pipeline tag; a download by its name reaches it.

**An image model cannot chat.** The proxy lists an image model with the
`image_generation` capability and no `context_window`, and refuses a chat
completion naming one with 400 `image_model_cannot_chat`, before admission.
The runtime's launch refuses one first, before it looks for files or stops a
resident. `not_an_image_model` has no writer in this change, since an image
model sent to `/v1/embeddings` is already refused as `not_an_embedding_model`;
it is deferred to the change that adds `POST /v1/images/generations`.

**Not covered.** No real Hub download: every download test runs on fakes.
sd.cpp loading any file gglib links, and how a model's kind of runtime is
modelled, belong to the change that launches it. No SDXL GGUF was measured,
and the import takes GGUF only, so the SDXL checkpoint, a safetensors file,
cannot be added yet; whether `-m` may take one is decided with the launch.
Diffusers directories are out of scope. The update check does not cover
companions. The native downloader skips a file already present
(`run_native` in `crates/gglib-download/src/executor/mod.rs`). The Rust side
runs the `hf_xet` accelerator, when provisioned, before that presence check,
and the helper calls `hf_hub_download(local_dir=dest_root,
force_download=args.force)`
(`crates/gglib-download/scripts/hf_xet_downloader.py`), so whether a present
companion is fetched again under the accelerator is `huggingface_hub`'s
local-dir decision. The paired machine's rows and
inspector show no Draws chip, and its detail carries a component's role and
presence but not its path. No page was looked at in a browser.

## 2026-10-10: gglib installs sd-server, launches an image model beside a chat model, and gives the GPU in turns

Interim decisions of the change that runs stable-diffusion.cpp: installing
its `sd-server`, launching an image model through it, placing that model in
the second slot, the generation gate between a render and the chats, and the
surfaces for all of it (`gglib config sd`, `gglib serve <image model>`, the
daemon's `install-sd`, `sd-status` and `uninstall-sd` routes, the proxy's
load, Settings and the Serve modal). Its commits, named by subject because
a hash changes at every rewrite, are "one GitHub-release installer,
parameterised by a release spec", "install stable-diffusion.cpp's
sd-server, pinned", "a model's runtime is derived, and sd-server has a
command line", "health is asked per runtime", "an image runtime's refusals
have codes", "every chat door refuses an image model by name", "an image
model takes the second slot", "the generation gate", "residency launches an
image model through sd-server", "config sd, and serve an image model", "the
image runtime on the daemon, its load and its status" and "Settings 'Image
runtime', and the Serve modal for an image model", in that order. File
references are to the commit this entry lands in. Nothing in this change
draws a picture: rendering through the gate is the next change's.

**The release and its assets.** The pin is `master-948-228c707`
(`PINNED_SD_RELEASE`, `crates/gglib-runtime/src/sd/release.rs`), published
upstream when it was read on 2026-10-10, overridable by `GGLIB_SD_RELEASE`
(a tag, or `latest`). Its assets are flat zips named after the build
runner's OS version (`sd-master-228c707-bin-Darwin-macOS-26.6.2-arm64.zip`,
`…-bin-Linux-Ubuntu-24.04-x86_64{,-vulkan,-rocm-7.14.0}.zip`,
`…-bin-win-{cpu,cuda12,vulkan,rocm-7.14.0}-x64.zip` and
`cudart-sd-bin-win-cu12-x64.zip`), so a loose pattern could match two, and
the install takes exactly one or refuses, naming the matches. The macOS zip
holds `sd-server`, `sd-cli`, `libstable-diffusion.dylib` and two licence
texts; the binaries are universal (x86_64 and arm64), link `Metal.framework`,
and load the dylib from beside themselves, which the install therefore
requires. The platform table: macOS on either arch takes that zip; Linux
x86_64 takes the Vulkan build when a Vulkan runtime is present, else the
CPU build, with a warning that images will take minutes each; Windows x64
takes CUDA 12 (with the cudart zip), else Vulkan, else the CPU build, with
the same warning. Anything else has no pre-built build, and `gglib config
sd install` builds from source there (`--build` forces it anywhere): `-DSD_METAL`, `-DSD_CUDA` or `-DSD_VULKAN` by
the detected acceleration, the frontend off, `--target sd-server`.
Everything lives under `.sd/`, and the download directory is
`.sd/downloads`, so a llama.cpp install, which removes its own download
directory whole, never removes it.

**The macOS asset is not slower than a source build, so it is the
default.** One run of each `sd-cli`, the asset's and the owner's source
build at `228c707` (built with `SD_METAL=ON`), with Flux schnell q8_0 at
1024², 4 steps, `euler`, seed 42, on 2026-10-10 between 10:53 and 10:56,
with the owner's yes, on a throwaway folder and no gglib binary. The owner's
daemon kept its Qwen3.8-27B resident and idle, and agents were compiling in
other worktrees, so both absolute times run high against the 2026-10-09
table. Both logs read `auto-fit: --backend "diffusion=MTL0,te=MTL0,vae=MTL0"`
and `total params memory size = 21687.45MB (VRAM 21687.45MB, RAM 0.00MB)`.

| Build | Sampling | `generate_image` |
|---|---|---|
| Release asset | 73.47 s | 88.37 s |
| Source build | 82.06 s | 98.26 s |

The macOS row of the platform table stays. The asset's `--version` reads
`stable-diffusion.cpp version unknown, commit 228c707`, so the version a
status reports is the install record's, and the commit is read from the
line (`commit_of`, `crates/gglib-runtime/src/sd/status.rs`).

**sd-server answers its health probe while it renders.** sd-server has no
`/health`; `GET /v1/models` answers its one id, `sd-cpp-local`, without the
render's lock. So a runtime's health path is `RuntimeKind::health_path`
(`/health` for llama-server, `/v1/models` for sd-server), and an sd answer
is ready only when its body names `sd-cpp-local`, so a foreign server on the
port is not taken for it. Read on 2026-10-10 from 10:56:56 to 10:59:13,
same yes, same conditions, port 18299: the asset's `sd-server` with Flux
schnell q8_0 rendered one 1024² image through `POST /sdcpp/v1/img_gen` with
`preview: proj`, and `GET /v1/models` was asked throughout, about every
2.2 s (one probe and one job poll a cycle, with a 2 s sleep between). All 61
probes answered 200, 60 while the job reported generating and the last as
it completed; the slowest took 213 ms. The body was checked once, after the
last probe, and named `sd-cpp-local`. The job reported `preview {step,
total_steps}` from 1/4 to 4/4, with frames of 56 to 61 KB of base64, and no
step before the first finished, about 40 s in (the server's log: 6.20 s of
prompt encoding, 3.09 s loading the diffusion weights, 30.57 s for step 1):
loading and prompt encoding report nothing, so a progress display has a
loading stage before step 1. The server's own timing was
119.06 s sampling, 9.54 s decode and 134.81 s in all, against the CLI's
73.47 s without previews under the same load; what the previews cost is not
separable from that load here.

The probes, the two `sd-cli` logs, the server's log, the run script and its
output are kept on the owner's machine in
`~/.local/src/handoff-2026-10-09-image-gen/gpu-2026-10-10/` and not in the
repository, so these numbers cannot be re-derived from a checkout.

**What an image model is budgeted at.** An image model's footprint in the
placement is its files plus a compute margin per family,
`Recipe::compute_margin_bytes`
(`crates/gglib-core/src/domain/image_family.rs`): 7 GiB for Flux.1, 8 GiB
for SDXL, 9 GiB for Qwen-Image 2.1. Each is the peak compute buffer of the
2026-10-09 runs at 1024², which is the VAE decode, rounded up: 6,657 MB,
7,680 MB and 8,650 MB. sd.cpp maps weights lazily, so the free-memory
reading lags a freshly launched image model; whether to pass `--eager-load`
is left for a reading of its own.

**Where an image model sits.** An image model never takes an empty primary.
It goes to the second slot when the memory check grants it, with no
`SECONDARY_MAX_BYTES` ceiling (the ceiling is for a small auxiliary chat
model; `decide_secondary_slot_for`, `crates/gglib-core/src/domain/residency.rs`),
or when the primary is empty, since there is nothing to share memory with;
otherwise it swaps into an evictable primary as any model does. A held
primary with no grant refuses at once with `image_model_does_not_fit`
(`Refusal::HeldSlot`, `choose_image_slot`,
`crates/gglib-runtime/src/process/admission/state_placement.rs`), and a
waiter whose only places are held slots no longer holds back a younger
request for another model.

**The gate as built.** Rules from the plan, each with where it lives
(`crates/gglib-runtime/src/process/admission/`):

1. *What counts.* LLM turns in flight are the explicit LLM turns plus every
   in-flight request on a llama-server resident (`llm_inflight`,
   `state_gate.rs`); a lease on an sd-server resident never counts.
2. *Who.* In this change only proxy leases on llama-server residents are LLM
   turns. `GenerationGate::llm_turn` and `render_turn`
   (`crates/gglib-core/src/ports/generation_gate.rs`) have no caller yet.
3. *Order.* A render starts when no render is held, no LLM turn is in
   flight and no gate waiter arrived before it (`render_may_start`). A
   llama-server serve or launch, and an LLM turn, go only when no render is
   held and no render waiter arrived first (`gate_admits_llm`, consulted by
   `may_serve` and by the launch path in `state.rs`). Tickets waiting for a
   slot are not turns and never hold back a render.
4. *Progress.* A render step is queue progress (`AdmissionRelease::progress`,
   `lease.rs`), and a gate waiter expires under the same three-minute stall
   rule as a ticket (`gate_stalled`).
5. and 6. are the placement above.
7. *Teardown.* `ProcessManager::retire_render` kills the process, then under
   one lock empties the slot, which settles the render's lease count, only
   if it still holds that model (`QueueState::retire`, `state_gate.rs`), then
   ends the turn with the lease disarmed (`AdmissionQueue::retire_render`,
   `gate.rs`).
8. *Observer.* A waiting LLM turn tells its observer the render's step,
   total and its own place, outside the lock, and again only when that
   changes (`wait_for_turn`, `gate.rs`).

A render turn owns its sd-server lease, so a detached task keeps both by
keeping one value. `/v1/proxy/status` shows the gate as
`admission.generation`: the render holding it (its image model, step and
total), the LLM turns in flight and the callers waiting for a turn.

**Where the plan and the code disagreed, resolved.**

- *D1.* "Components checked before any eviction" cannot hold inside the
  launch: by then the queue has forgotten the resident it displaces. The
  check is a preflight in `ResidentSet::admit`, before the queue (binary,
  missing roles, each component's file; `preflight` in
  `crates/gglib-runtime/src/process/residency/launch_files.rs`), and the
  launch stops the displaced resident first, then checks the files again, so
  a late refusal never leaves a server running unknown to the queue
  (`a_launch_whose_files_vanished_still_stops_the_displaced_model`,
  `launch_sd_tests.rs`).
- *D2.* With the launch refusal the entry above describes gone, each
  chat door refuses an image model by name with 400
  `image_model_cannot_chat` before it admits anything: hub turns, `gglib
  chat` and `gglib q`, and the web agent run, through one predicate
  (`refuse_unless_chats`, `crates/gglib-core/src/request_pipeline/chat_runtime.rs`).
- *D3.* The orphan sweep recognises `sd-server` as well as llama-server
  (`is_our_server`, `crates/gglib-runtime/src/pidfile/verify.rs`); an
  orphaned sd-server holds the whole model.
- *D4.* `launched_context` needed no change; it matches port and id. The
  primary is still what `current_model` reports whatever serves it, and the
  readers that speak llama-server's own API (the `/slots` poller, the
  baseline read, `/v1/models`' `context_window`, the hub's latest server)
  skip a target whose runtime is sd-server.
- *D5.* Where "always the secondary" met "no headroom swaps", an image
  model swaps into an evictable chat primary; it never takes an empty one.

A person's Stop names a model, not a slot: `ModelRuntimePort::stop_model`
is required of every runtime, so the web Stop reaches an image model in the
second slot and leaves the chat model running. A load of an image model
answers context 0 and is not taken for a llama-server restart.

**Not covered.** No gglib binary launched a real sd-server: every launch
test runs a stand-in script, and the readings above ran sd.cpp's own
binaries. No CUDA, Vulkan or ROCm asset was installed or run; their rows are
matched against the 2026-10-10 asset names only. No 1536² reading, so the
margins hold for 1024². Lazy mapping against the live free-memory reading
was not measured. The gate has no explicit turn taker yet, so a render and
a chat have only been ordered in queue tests. The CLI dashboard's slot line
does not yet name a slot's runtime or family, and the status carries no
family per slot. No page was looked at in a browser.
